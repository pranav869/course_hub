❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQS)
1. How are course fees processed securely? ▾
All course fees on CourseHub Learning Portal are processed through the official Razorpay payment gateway. We support UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Net Banking across all Indian nationalized and private banks, and all RuPay/Visa/MasterCard cards. Signatures are verified cryptographically using server-side HMAC-SHA256 protocols.
2. When will I receive my payment receipt? ▾
An electronic receipt bearing a unique computer-generated Receipt Number (e.g. CH-RCP-2026-XXXXXX) and Razorpay Payment ID is generated immediately on your screen upon transaction confirmation. You can also view and print receipts at any time under the Fee History tab.
3. How is the course completion certificate verified? ▾
Upon finishing 80% or more of lecture units, learners can generate their verified digital certificate. Each credential contains a unique Certificate ID and a verification QR code that can be authenticated by employers or academic institutions.
4. What should I do if money is deducted but enrollment is pending? ▾
If your bank account was debited but the course did not activate immediately, please allow 15 minutes for bank webhook reconciliation. Alternatively, lodge a query on this Helpdesk page with your Razorpay Payment ID.
5. Can I access lecture videos on mobile devices? ▾
Yes, the CourseHub LMS is fully responsive across all standard Android, iOS, tablet, and desktop browser environments.
